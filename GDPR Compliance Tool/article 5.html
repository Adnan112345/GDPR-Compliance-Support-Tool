Article 5 Training Module

Article 5 sets out the key principles organisations must follow when processing personal data. Read each section and answer the question to track your progress.

1. Lawfulness, Fairness and Transparency

Learn: Personal data must be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently.

Example: A company explains in its privacy notice why it collects customer email addresses.

Question: Which option best shows transparency?

2. Purpose Limitation

Learn: Data should only be collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes.

Example: Contact details collected for enrolment should not later be used for unrelated marketing.

Question: A gym collects phone numbers for emergencies and later uses them for promotions. What principle is at risk?

3. Data Minimisation

Learn: Only collect the personal data that is necessary for the purpose.

Example: A newsletter form asks only for an email address, not extra unnecessary details.

Question: Which option best follows data minimisation?

4. Accuracy

Learn: Personal data must be accurate and kept up to date where necessary.

Example: An employee updates their address and the employer corrects the record promptly.

Question: Keeping outdated emergency contact details after being told of a change affects which principle?

5. Storage Limitation

Learn: Personal data should not be kept for longer than necessary.

Example: A company deletes unsuccessful job applications after its retention period expires.

Question: Why is keeping data too long a risk?

6. Integrity and Confidentiality

Learn: Personal data must be kept secure using appropriate technical and organisational measures.

Example: A business uses encryption, strong passwords, and access controls for HR files.

Question: Which option best supports this principle?

7. Accountability

Learn: Organisations must be able to demonstrate compliance with GDPR principles.

Example: A company keeps training records, policies, audits, and lawful basis documentation.

Question: Which option best shows accountability?

8. Privacy Notices

Learn: A privacy notice supports transparency by explaining what data is collected, why it is used, and people’s rights.

Question: What is the main purpose of a privacy notice?

9. Retention and Deletion

Learn: Organisations should have clear retention periods and deletion processes.

Question: What is the main benefit of having a retention schedule?

10. Staff Awareness

Learn: Staff training helps organisations apply the principles consistently in daily practice.
